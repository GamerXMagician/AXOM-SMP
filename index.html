Server Rules

📜 AXOM SMP Server Rules

Welcome to AXOM SMP! ⚔️🔥 Please follow these rules to keep the server fair, fun, and enjoyable for everyone.

🌍

General

🤝Respect Everyone

Treat all players with respect. No bullying, harassment, or unnecessary toxicity.

🚫No Spamming

Do not spam messages, commands, mentions, or emojis.

📢No Advertising

Do not advertise other servers, Discord servers, or websites without permission.

⚔️

Minecraft

🛠️No Cheating

Hacked clients, X-Ray, unfair mods, autoclickers, and other cheats are not allowed.

🧨No Exploiting

Do not abuse glitches, bugs, duplication glitches, or unintended mechanics.

🏠No Griefing

Do not destroy, steal from, or damage another player's builds without permission.

💰No Scamming

Do not trick players during trades or deals.

⚔️Fair PvP

PvP must follow the server's Lifesteal and event rules.

🔥No Unnecessary Destruction

Do not intentionally destroy large areas of the world or ruin other players' gameplay.

🛡️

Staff

👑Follow Staff Instructions

Respect staff decisions and follow instructions when required.

🎫Use Tickets for Help

For reports, appeals, or problems, use the appropriate ticket channel.

📖Keep Updated

Rules may be changed or updated by the AXOM SMP staff when necessary.

⚠️ Breaking the rules may result in a warning, mute, kick, temporary ban, or permanent ban.
🔥 Play fair. Respect others. Build your legacy.
