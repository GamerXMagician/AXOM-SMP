About the server

Built for players who take their survival seriously.

AXOM SMP is a Minecraft Lifesteal Survival server where every fight has a permanent cost — and every victory makes you stronger. Build bases, form alliances, betray them, and carve out your own story across a world shared with a close, competitive community.

Whether you're here to build sprawling kingdoms, hunt other players for their hearts, or just survive quietly on the edge of the map, AXOM SMP gives you a persistent world worth fighting for.

Server Features

Lifesteal
